01 / The process

How it works

Required: a link to your Pinterest board, plus a few sentences on your space and what you're hoping changes.

Optional: photos of the current space

I build your guide

I study your board and put together a private page like the example above, your style broken down, a palette, and curated picks, plus a short video walking through my choices.

02 / The fine print

What's included

Included

  • A private guide page built for your space
  • A style breakdown based on your board
  • A recommended palette
  • Curated picks: thrift, DIY, splurge, buy
  • A short video walking through my choices

Not included (yet)

  • Virtual room mockups or renderings
  • On-site or in-person visits
  • Full space planning or architectural changes

I'm not a licensed interior designer, I provide personal interior decorating advice, not architectural or professional design services.
