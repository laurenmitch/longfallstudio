01 / Roughly how it'd go

How it works

You send me your inspo

A Pinterest board, a handful of saved images, whatever you've been collecting, plus a few sentences about your space and what you wish were different.

I build your guide

I go through your inspo and put together a page like the example above: your style broken down, a palette, and curated picks across thrift / DIY / splurge / buy, plus a short video walking through my choices.

You bring it to life

You get a link to keep and revisit whenever you're ready to shop, thrift, or DIY.

02 / Say hi

Let me know you're interested

Only the email is required for now, and we can sort out the details from there. But it'd be great to hear a bit about your space and your style!

How you'd describe your style, and a bit about your space, what's missing, what you wish it felt like.
If you have a board handy, drop the link. Totally fine to skip and send it later.
