Guide 06

Warm Modern

This home is modern, clean, and intentional, but I wouldn't call it minimalist. It's colorful and simple on the surface, but look closer and you'll spot geometric sculptural lamps, folk art hung next to bold posters, and built-ins packed with books. It feels warm, like a professor's home, but one where the professor has a hot, much-younger art-collector wife.

Scroll for the full plan ↓

01 / Your style

How I'd describe it

What it is

  • A bold modern
  • Raw, light wood tones
  • Unique modern furniture with vintage and folk art mixed in
  • Full of life, color, and objects

What it isn't

  • A beige modern
  • Dark & heavy, overly ornate
  • A matching set that all arrived at once
  • Minimalist or sparse
