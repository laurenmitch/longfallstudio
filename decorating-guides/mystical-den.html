Guide 03

Mystical Den

It's a botanical fantasy with Art Nouveau influences, lilac sheets, and a dedicated writing space. Your space is ornate but unpretentious, and possibly has a subtle scent of incense. Your bed serves as a centerpiece in your space, with layers of color and texture that you can melt into.

01 / Your style

How i'd describe it

What it is

  • A home fit for a fairy
  • ethereal color choices and lighting
  • objects with a story
  • designed to elicit good sleep and even better dreams
  • Bringing the outdoors in, fresh oxygen filled air

What it isn't

  • A home fit for a tech bro
  • cold and orderly
  • mass produced / generic
  • a perfectly made bed
  • stuffy, closed windows, dark

05 / Make it

What to DIY

DIY

Thrifted Frames + Public Domain Art Nouveau Posters

Notes

I'd say thrift the biggest, nicest, most ornate frame you can find and then get a real vintage public domain art nouveau poster printed and put it in there. If you are able to find a tall and skinny frame, I feel like there are a lot of great images with that aspect ratio.

Reddit says https://thestackhouse.com/ is a good place to get the printing done.
