Guide 05

Polished Townhome

When you step into this home it smells good, the floors squeak they are so clean... an adult lives here. Your style is refined but not precious, with European influences filtered through a distinctly American lens (East Coast). You love subtle floral patterns, feminine colors, and interiors that feel a little bit old fashioned. In a word, classic.

Scroll for the full plan ↓

01 / Your style

How i'd describe it

What it is

  • Cohesive & simple
  • stripes, florals, diagonal checkered pattern
  • Old-world charm
  • Refined but still lived-in

What it isn't

  • Flashy statement pieces
  • polka dots, gingham, wavy checkered pattern
  • A historic house tour
  • Stuffy and dusty
