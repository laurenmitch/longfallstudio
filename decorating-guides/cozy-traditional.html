Guide 01

Cozy Traditional

Let us waltz into your Nancy Meyers dream house. I'd define your style as traditional meets transitional, but not in a boomer way. It feels proper, and feminine, and won't be going out of style anytime soon. Sometimes it leans a little cottage, and other times it feels like an art collector's city apartment. I think we can pull off doing a bit of both.

Scroll for the full plan ↓

01 / Your style

How i'd describe it

What it is

  • Timelessly fashionable, "classic"
  • gallery walls full of original artwork
  • Eclectic and lived-in, warm
  • traditional looking lamps, large-scale flower arrangements
  • slip covers

What it isn't

  • trendy or trying to reference a specific era
  • gallery walls full of digital art / posters
  • minimalist, sterile
  • Modern statement lamps, anything "edgy"
  • leather

Persian Rugs

05 / Make it

What to DIY

DIY

Ottoman Slip Cover

Notes

It's hard to find an ottoman with the exact right funky-fabric. Since you just got a sewing machine AND you are into the slip cover look, I feel like this would be perfect!

Consider thrifting a coffee-table sized ottoman and covering it up. Check out matchy-matchy sewing club for fabric.
