Guide 04

Bold Naturalist

When you come home after a big trip and drop your bag on the floor you want to look around at a home that is whimsical, saturated, and full of collected treasures! It's unapologetically colorful and zoological, and we're going to get there with thoughtfully paired colors, bold tile, and WILD wallpaper choices.

Scroll for the full plan ↓

01 / Your style

How i'd describe it

What it is

  • dreamy, inviting you to explore
  • creative references to science, bugs, stars, and animals
  • Fearless complementary color combos

What it isn't

  • dizzying, overwhelming with color and stuff
  • MINIMALIST
  • Shades of beige
