Urban Cottage

Snow White comes home from a long day at the office, kicks off her pumps, and puts on a kettle. It's whimsical, unpretentious, and cozy. It's possible you got most of your furniture from your grandparents.

01 / Your style

How i'd describe it

What it is

  • lived in
  • folk art, objects with a story, hand-painted
  • an intuitively collected mix of old and new
  • wood & ceramic

What it isn't

  • cluttered
  • digital, crisp, reproduced
  • Fully polished & modern (or only antique!)
  • plastic & metal

05 / Make it

What to DIY

DIY

Re-knob a dresser

Notes

Thrift (or buy) some vintage knobs and re-knob your dresser. You could have all the knobs be different, or keep to a certain style like all blue or star shaped (like the link).

Shop supplies →
DIY

Paint switch covers

Notes

This comes directly from your board and feels like the PERFECT way to add the hand-painted folk art touch. Maybe have Sarah over to help with the painting.

You could buy plastic covers, paint a base coat of white, then paint over them, and seal with polyurethane.
