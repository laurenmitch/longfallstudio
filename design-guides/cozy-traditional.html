Guide 01

Cozy Traditional

Let us waltz into your Nancy Meyers dream house. I'd define your style as traditional meets transitional, with none of the boomer mom baggage. It feels proper, and feminine, and won't be going out of style anytime soon. Sometimes it leans a little cottage, and other times it feels like an art collector's city apartment. I think we can pull off doing a bit of both.

Scroll for the full plan ↓

01 / Your style

How I'd describe it

What it is

  • Timelessly fashionable, leaning on classic traditional elements
  • gallery walls full of original artwork
  • Eclectic and lived-in, warm
  • Ample traditional looking lamps, large-scale flower arrangements
  • slip covers

What it isn't

  • trendy or trying to reference a specific era
  • gallery walls full of digital art / posters
  • minimalist, sterlie
  • Modern statement lamps, anything "edgy"
  • leather

What to thrift

DIY

Notes

Consider thrifting a coffee-table sized ottoman and covering it up. Check out matchy-matchy sewing club for fabric.
