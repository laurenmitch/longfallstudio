05 / Make it

What to DIY

DIY

Re-knob a dresser

Notes

Thrift (or buy) some vintage knobs and re-knob your dresser. You could have all the knobs be different, or keep to a certain style like all blue or all flower shaped (like the example).

DIY

Paint switch covers

Notes

This comes directly from your board and feels like the PERFECT way to add the hand-painted folk art touch. Maybe have Sarah over to help with the painting.

You could buy plastic covers, paint a base coat of white, then paint over them, and seal with polyurethane.

DIY

Self expression fridge

Notes

Your fridge is now your tumblr. I saw this a few times in your inspo and I love it. For once in your life you have your fridge completely to yourself, and it should be a reflection of you.

DIY

Peel and Stick Gel Backsplash

Notes

You have the perfect space for a tiled backsplash! I would probably attach these to something like acrylic or thin plywood first, then lightly attach that to the wall with strong double sided tape rather than sticking them directly to the wall, just to make move-out less stressful.
