01 / Your style

How I'd describe it

What it is

  • Light woods: ash, birch, pine
  • Warm neutrals with one or two clear colors
  • Soft, layered textiles: wool, linen, sheepskin
  • Simple forms, honest materials
  • Plants and handmade ceramics

What it isn't

  • Cold, all-white, and sterile
  • Flat-pack everything with no soul
  • Matchy sets bought in one trip
  • Fussy, ornate, or heavy
  • Trend-chasing that dates in a year
